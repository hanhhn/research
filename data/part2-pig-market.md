## 3. African Swine Fever (ASF) Impact in Vietnam

**Status:** Partial data retrieved

**Key Findings:**
- **Year of Major Outbreak:** 2019
- **Estimated Losses:** Approximately **5-6 million pigs** culled or died from ASF
- **First Detection:** February 2019 in northern Vietnam
- **Spread:** Rapidly spread across all 63 provinces
- **Population Loss:** Approximately **20%** of Vietnam's total pig population lost
- **Economic Impact:** Estimated losses of hundreds of millions of USD

**Recovery Status:**
- Recovery efforts ongoing since 2019-2020
- Herd rebuilding progressing through 2022-2024
- Population reduced by 20-25% due to ASF (2019-2022)
- Current recovery showing positive trends

**Data Source:** Internal feasibility study - FarmTech Vietnam (2024)

**Sources:**
- FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization) ASF reports
- OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health) reports
- Vietnam Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development (MARD) announcements
- News reports from 2019 (Reuters, BBC, etc.)

**Note:** Specific verified figures require consultation of official FAO/OIE reports.

## 4. Vietnam Pig Farm Structure

**Status:** Detailed data available

**Farm Size Distribution:**
- **Household Farms (< 50 pigs):** Approximately **60%** of total production
- **Small Farms (50-500 pigs):** Approximately **25%** of total production
- **Commercial/Industrial Farms (> 500 pigs):** Approximately **15%** of total production (growing rapidly)

**Characteristics:**
- Highly fragmented sector dominated by small-scale producers
- Most rural households keep pigs for supplementary income and food security
- Gradual shift toward larger commercial operations
- Industrial farms showing fastest growth rate

**Key Regions:**
- Mekong Delta (major production area)
- Southeast Region
- Red River Delta

## 7. Pig Farming Costs Breakdown

**Status:** General cost structure identified

**Feed Cost Percentage:**
- **65-70% of total production costs** (based on industry data)

**Comparison with Other Sectors:**
- Higher than shrimp farming (50-65%)
- Consistent with global swine industry standards

**Note:** Updated based on Vietnam-specific industry data from internal research

**Typical Cost Breakdown:**
1. **Feed costs:** 60-75% of total costs
2. **Piglets (breeding stock):** 15-20% of total costs
3. **Veterinary and medicine:** 3-5% of total costs
4. **Labor:** 5-8% of total costs
5. **Utilities and facilities:** 3-5% of total costs
6. **Other miscellaneous costs:** 2-4% of total costs

**Key Feed Ingredients:**
- Corn
- Soybean meal
- Rice bran
- Cassava

**Cost Factors:**
- Scale of operation (smallholder vs. commercial)
- Geographic location (Mekong Delta, Northern Highlands, etc.)
- Feed price volatility (import dependence on soybean and corn)

**Sources to Verify:**
- Vietnam Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development reports
- FAO country reports on swine production economics
- Recent agricultural economics research papers
