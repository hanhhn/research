## 6. Shrimp Farming Costs Breakdown

### Status: NO SPECIFIC DATA RETRIEVED

**Typical Cost Structure (Industry General Knowledge):**

#### Feed Costs: 50-65% of total production costs
- Commercial shrimp feed (largest expense)
- Varies by farming intensity
- Dependent on global feed ingredient prices

#### Seed/Post-Larvae: 10-15% of total costs
- Hatchery quality critical
- Price varies by species and size
- Health certification important

#### Labor: 8-12% of total costs
- Varies by farm size and technology level
- Intensive systems require more skilled labor
- Harvest labor costs

#### Other Major Expenses:
- **Energy/Fuel:** 5-8% (aeration, water pumping)
- **Chemicals and Probiotics:** 3-5%
- **Equipment and Infrastructure:** Depreciation
- **Medicines and Health Management:** 2-4%
- **Water Treatment:** Variable

**Variables Affecting Costs:**

## 7. Common Shrimp Diseases in Vietnam

### Status: NO SPECIFIC DATA RETRIEVED

**Major Disease Threats:**

#### White Spot Syndrome Virus (WSSV)
- Most devastating viral disease
- High mortality rates
- Significant historical economic impact
- Biosecurity critical for prevention

#### Early Mortality Syndrome (EMS) / Acute Hepatopancreatic Necrosis Disease (AHPND)
- Bacterial disease (Vibrio parahaemolyticus)
- Major outbreaks in 2010s
- Caused massive losses
- Ongoing management challenge

#### Other Notable Diseases:
- Infectious Hypodermal and Hematopoietic Necrosis Virus (IHHNV)
- Taura Syndrome Virus (TSV)
- Yellow Head Virus (YHV)
- Enterocytozoon hepatopenaei (EHP) - microsporidian parasite

**Economic Impact:**
- Historical outbreaks caused billions in losses
- Ongoing production risk
- Management costs significant
- Insurance availability limited

**Disease Management:**
- Biosecurity protocols
- Improved seed quality
- Better water management
- Probiotic use
- Vaccination research ongoing

**Data Sources Needed:**
- Vietnam Research Institute for Aquaculture
- International aquatic animal health reports
- OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health) reports
