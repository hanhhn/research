- "farm management software pricing subscription 2024"

**Result:** All pricing queries hit rate limits.

**General Pricing Models (Based on Industry Knowledge - Requires Verification):**

### Common Pricing Structures:

**1. Per Acre/Hectare Model:**
- Typical range: $0.50 - $5+ per acre/hectare
- Scales with farm size
- Examples: Climate FieldView (historically used this model)

**2. Per User/Seat Model:**
- Basic: 1 user included
- Additional users: $10-50+ per user/month
- Common for collaborative farm management platforms

**3. Tiered Subscription Plans:**

| Tier | Price Range | Features |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Basic/Starter | $10-50/month | Record keeping, basic tracking |
| Professional | $50-150/month | Analytics, inventory, financials |
| Enterprise | $150-500+/month | Full suite, satellite, IoT, multi-farm |

**4. Freemium Model:**
- Free tier: Basic features, limited records/users
- Paid tiers: Advanced features, unlimited usage
- Examples: Some apps offer free basic record-keeping

**5. Per Feature/Add-on Model:**
- Base platform fee
- Additional modules: satellite imagery, IoT integration, advanced analytics
- Common in enterprise solutions
